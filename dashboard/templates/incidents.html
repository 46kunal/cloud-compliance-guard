When something goes wrong, the clocks start. Cyber incidents of the types in CERT-In Annexure I must be reported to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing them. For a personal data breach, DPDP Rule 7 also asks for the Data Protection Board and affected people to be told without delay, with a detailed report to the Board within 72 hours. PolicyGuard tracks the clocks and drafts the text. A person reviews and files it: nothing is sent automatically.
